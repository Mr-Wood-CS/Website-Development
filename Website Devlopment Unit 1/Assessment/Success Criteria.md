# Success Criteria

## Must

- I created a one-page website.
- I used RocketCake.
- I included a title and introduction.
- I included at least three content sections.
- I saved my work correctly.

## Should

- I used researched facts in my own words.
- I included suitable images.
- I recorded my sources.
- I checked spelling and readability.
- I tested the website against the brief.

## Could

- I improved layout for different devices.
- I used navigation or clear section order.
- I explained how structured information appears in my page.
- I explained how a browser loads information from a webpage.
