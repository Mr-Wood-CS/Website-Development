# Rubric

| Area | Developing | Secure | Exceeding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planning | Basic audience or purpose is recorded | Clear audience, purpose and section plan | Detailed plan explains why sections suit the audience |
| Research | Limited facts or missing sources | Useful facts are recorded with sources | Facts are well selected, rewritten and clearly linked to user needs |
| Website build | Page is incomplete or difficult to follow | One-page RocketCake site has title, sections and images | Page is polished, consistent and easy to scan |
| Information structure | Some content is present but weakly organised | Content is organised into logical sections | Structure helps users compare and understand information quickly |
| Testing | Limited checking | Tests are completed and fixes are recorded | Testing includes peer feedback, accessibility and device checks |
| Evaluation | Simple opinion | Evaluation explains strengths and improvements | Evaluation uses evidence and computing vocabulary confidently |
| Computing understanding | Limited use of key words | Explains webpages, browsers or structured information | Links website work to information systems, representation and networks |

## Teacher Judgement

Use professional judgement across the evidence. A pupil does not need to be exceeding in every area to demonstrate strong overall performance.
