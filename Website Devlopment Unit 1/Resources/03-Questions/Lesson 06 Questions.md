# Lesson 06 Questions

## Starter Questions (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. What is evaluation?
2. What is feedback?

### 2 - Making progress
1. What evidence can show your website meets the brief?
2. Why is feedback useful before final submission?

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain one strength of your website with evidence.
2. Explain one area for improvement with evidence.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Evaluate your website against success criteria and justify your overall judgement.
2. Explain how your final design choices connect to audience and purpose.

## Check for Understanding (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. Name one strength of your website.
2. Name one target for improvement.

### 2 - Making progress
1. Match one piece of evidence to one success criterion.
2. Choose one computing word that explains your design work.

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain how one piece of evidence demonstrates quality.
2. Explain how your testing improved the final version.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Evaluate whether your evidence is sufficient and identify any gaps.
2. Justify one improvement target for a future project.

## Exit Ticket (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
Complete: Next time I would improve ___.

### 2 - Making progress
Complete: Next time I would improve ___ because ___.

### 3 - Making Good progress
Complete: Next time I would improve ___ because ___. I would do this by ___.

### 4 - Meeting target
Complete: Next time I would improve ___ because ___. I would do this by ___. This would improve user experience by ___.
