Planning a One Page Website

Today's Lesson

Planning a One Page Website.

Learning Intention

Success Criteria

We will explore how to organise a website so that visitors find the information they need quickly and easily.

  • Identify the target audience and the website's main purpose.
  • Choose the best sections for a one-page design.
  • Create a clear page plan with headings and content order.

Key Words

Audience

Who the website is designed for, such as tourists or families.

Purpose

Why the site exists: to inform, persuade, help visitors, or encourage booking.

Section

A block of related content, for example attractions, transport or tips.

Layout

How the page is arranged so it is easy to read and use.

Information

The facts, details and messages the visitor needs.
