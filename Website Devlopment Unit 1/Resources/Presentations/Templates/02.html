Today's Lesson

Planning a One Page Website.

Learning Intention

We will explore how to organise a website so that visitors find the information they need quickly and easily.

Success Criteria

  • Identify the target audience and the website's main purpose.
  • Choose the best sections for a one-page design.
  • Create a clear page plan with headings and content order.

Your goal: build a page plan that is clear, useful, and easy to follow.
