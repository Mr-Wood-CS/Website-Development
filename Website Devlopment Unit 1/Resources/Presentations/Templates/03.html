Key Words

Words we will use today

Audience

Who the website is designed for, such as tourists or families.

Purpose

Why the site exists: to inform, persuade, help visitors, or encourage booking.

Section

A block of related content, for example attractions, transport or tips.

Layout

How the page is arranged so it is easy to read and use.

Information

The facts, details and messages the visitor needs.

Use these words when explaining your page plan.
