# Lesson 04 Questions

## Starter Questions (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. Why can a webpage look different on different devices?
2. What is a heading?

### 2 - Making progress
1. What makes a heading useful?
2. Why is section order important?

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain how layout affects user understanding.
2. Explain why whitespace can improve readability.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Compare the same page on two devices and explain two differences.
2. Recommend one layout change and justify it for your audience.

## Check for Understanding (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. What does a browser do?
2. Name one part of a web address.

### 2 - Making progress
1. Give two ways the same information could be represented on a webpage.
2. Define sender and receiver.

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain sender, receiver, address and packets in simple terms.
2. Explain how these ideas relate to loading your webpage.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Explain how structured information and network processes both affect user experience.
2. Evaluate one navigation choice in your page and justify whether it should stay or change.

## Exit Ticket (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
Describe one layout change you made.

### 2 - Making progress
Describe one layout change and why you made it.

### 3 - Making Good progress
Describe one layout change and explain how it helped readability.

### 4 - Meeting target
Describe one layout change and evaluate its impact on users on different devices.
