# Lesson 04 Questions

## Starter Questions

1. Why can a webpage look different on different devices?
2. What makes a heading useful?
3. Why is section order important?

## Check for Understanding

1. Give two ways the same information could be represented on a webpage.
2. What does a browser do?
3. What are sender, receiver, address and packets?

## Exit Ticket

Describe one layout improvement you made and why.
