# Lesson 02 Questions

## Starter Questions

1. What makes a source trustworthy?
2. Why should you record where an image came from?
3. Why is copying and pasting large blocks of text a problem?

## Check for Understanding

1. What is an information model?
2. Give three characteristics that could describe a tourist attraction.
3. How could attractions be sorted or searched?

## Exit Ticket

Name one source you used and one fact you rewrote in your own words.
