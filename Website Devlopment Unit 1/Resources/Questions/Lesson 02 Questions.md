# Lesson 02 Questions

## Starter Questions (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. What is a source?
2. What does trustworthy mean?

### 2 - Making progress
1. Give one sign that a website source is trustworthy.
2. Why should you record where an image came from?

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain why copying large blocks of text is a problem.
2. Explain why facts should be rewritten in your own words.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Compare two sources and justify which is more reliable for your website.
2. Explain how source quality affects the final website.

## Check for Understanding (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. What is an information model?
2. Name one detail you could collect about an attraction.

### 2 - Making progress
1. Give three characteristics to describe a tourist attraction.
2. Put three attractions into alphabetical order.

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain one way attractions could be sorted.
2. Explain one way attractions could be searched.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Design a simple information model for attractions and explain why your fields are useful.
2. Explain how your model helps users compare attractions quickly.

## Exit Ticket (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
Name one source you used.

### 2 - Making progress
Name one source and one fact from it.

### 3 - Making Good progress
Name one source and rewrite one fact in your own words.

### 4 - Meeting target
Name one source, rewrite one fact in your own words, and justify why the source is trustworthy.
