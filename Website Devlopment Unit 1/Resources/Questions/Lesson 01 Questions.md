# Lesson 01 Questions

## Starter Questions

1. What is a website?
2. What is a webpage?
3. Why does a website need a clear audience?
4. What might a tourist need to know before visiting Edinburgh?

## Check for Understanding

1. Explain the difference between audience and purpose.
2. Why should important information be near the top of a page?
3. What sections would help a family visiting Edinburgh?

## Exit Ticket

Complete: My website is for ___ and its purpose is to ___.
