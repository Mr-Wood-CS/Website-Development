# Lesson 03 Questions

## Starter Questions (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. What is a visual development tool?
2. What is RocketCake used for?

### 2 - Making progress
1. Why do we preview a webpage while building?
2. Why should work be saved regularly?

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain one advantage of building from a plan.
2. Explain how drag-and-drop tools can reduce mistakes.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Justify why RocketCake is suitable for building a one-page website in this unit.
2. Explain one limitation of visual tools and how to manage it.

## Check for Understanding (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
1. Which RocketCake tool adds text?
2. Which RocketCake tool adds images?

### 2 - Making progress
1. Describe how to add a heading and a paragraph.
2. Describe how to place an image in the correct section.

### 3 - Making Good progress
1. Explain how layout choices can improve readability.
2. Explain why consistent spacing matters.

### 4 - Meeting target
1. Evaluate your current page against your plan and identify one precise improvement.
2. Explain how your build decisions support the target audience.

## Exit Ticket (Scaffolded)

### 1 - Begining to learn
Name one thing you added today.

### 2 - Making progress
Name two changes you made today.

### 3 - Making Good progress
Name two changes and explain why they improved your page.

### 4 - Meeting target
Name two changes, explain why they improved your page, and link each change to audience or purpose.
