Task

You are planning a one-page "Visit Edinburgh" website for tourists who want to quickly find useful trip information.

Choose the level that stretches your thinking

1 Beginning to learn
  1. Who is the audience for the "Visit Edinburgh" website?
  2. What is one purpose of the website?
2 Making progress
  1. Give one thing tourists would need to know before visiting Edinburgh.
  2. Name two sections that would help tourists use the website.
3 Making good progress
  1. Explain why the most useful tourist information should appear near the top of the page.
  2. Describe how clear headings would help visitors find Edinburgh information quickly.
4 Meeting target
  1. Suggest the best first section for the tourist website and justify your choice.
  2. Explain how your section order supports tourists who need quick trip information.

Start where you feel confident, then try the next level.
