Example Layout

A one-page website needs a clear journey

This wireframe shows how sections can be arranged in a logical order.

  1. 1
    Title

    A strong heading and image to welcome visitors.

  2. 2
    Introduction

    A short overview of what the page is about.

  3. 3
    Attractions

    Key places to visit in Edinburgh.

  4. 4
    Transport

    How to get to and around the city.

  5. 5
    Visitor tips

    Practical advice for a better trip.

  6. 6
    Credits

    Sources, image credits, and extra information.

Plan the order first, then build each section with a clear purpose.
