    Research Trial 6 - Complete Your Research Table

    Today You Will

    Put your research into one table.

    This table will help you use your research when you build your website.

    Finished Example

    Place Or Topic Useful Fact Source Website Source URL My Website Sentence
    Edinburgh Castle It has exhibitions, city views, and important Scottish history. VisitScotland https://www.visitscotland.com/ Edinburgh Castle is a historic place to visit, with exhibitions, city views, and important Scottish history.

    Each box has a job.

    Box What Goes In It
    Place Or Topic What you researched.
    Useful Fact The helpful information you found.
    Source Website The website where you found it.
    Source URL The exact web address.
    My Website Sentence Your rewritten sentence.

    Your Research Table

    Complete one row first. Then add more rows if you have time.

    Place Or Topic Useful Fact Source Website Source URL My Website Sentence

    Build Your Answer

    Question: How can a research table help you organise information?

    Use each row to build one clear answer.

    My Answer Needs To Words I Could Use
    Say what the table does A research table helps me organise information.
    Say what goes in it It can store useful facts, sources, source URLs, and rewritten sentences.
    Say why it helps It keeps my research in one place.
    Link it to building This makes it easier to choose what to include in my final website.

    Now Write Your Answer

    Question My Answer
    How can a research table help you organise information?

    Final Check

    Before you finish, check:

    • Did I choose information that helps a visitor?
    • Did I write down the website name?
    • Did I copy the source URL?
    • Did I rewrite the fact in my own words?
    • Would I trust the source?
