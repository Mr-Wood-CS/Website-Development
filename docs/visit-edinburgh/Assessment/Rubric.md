# Rubric

| Area | Level 1 Beginning | Level 2 Making Progress | Level 3 Secure | Level 4 Exceeding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planning | Basic audience or purpose is recorded | Audience and purpose are identified with a simple section plan | Clear audience, purpose and section plan | Detailed plan explains why sections suit the audience |
| Research | Limited facts are recorded or sources are missing | Some useful facts are recorded with at least one source | Useful facts are recorded with sources | Facts are well selected, rewritten and clearly linked to user needs |
| Website build | Page is incomplete or difficult to follow | Page has some useful content, headings or images | One-page RocketCake site has title, sections and images | Page is polished, consistent and easy to scan |
| Information structure | Some content is present but weakly organised | Content is grouped into simple sections | Content is organised into logical sections | Structure helps users compare and understand information quickly |
| Testing | Limited checking | Some checks are completed and at least one improvement is made | Tests are completed and fixes are recorded | Testing includes peer feedback, accessibility and device checks |
| Evaluation | Simple opinion | Evaluation describes at least one strength and one improvement | Evaluation explains strengths and improvements | Evaluation uses evidence and computing vocabulary confidently |
| Computing understanding | Limited use of key words | Uses some key words about webpages, browsers or information | Explains webpages, browsers or structured information | Links website work to information systems, representation and networks |

## Teacher Judgement

Use professional judgement across the evidence. A pupil does not need to be exceeding in every area to demonstrate strong overall performance.
