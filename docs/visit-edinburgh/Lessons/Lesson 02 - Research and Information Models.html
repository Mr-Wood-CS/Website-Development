Lesson 02 - Research and Information Models

Learning intention: I can gather reliable information and organise it into a simple model for a website.

Success criteria:

  • I can collect information from suitable sources.
  • I can record image and text sources.
  • I can describe the characteristics of each content item.

Starter

Which is more useful for a visitor: "Edinburgh is nice" or "Edinburgh Castle is open daily and is near the Royal Mile"? Explain why.

Teacher Input

Introduce an information model: item name, category, description, location, audience value, source and image.

Main Task

  1. Use the Research Log to collect facts for at least three attractions or visitor topics.
  2. Use the Asset Log to record images and permissions.
  3. Rewrite copied facts in your own words.
  4. Choose the best content for a one-page site.

Structured Support

Use the sentence starter: "This section is useful for visitors because..."

Challenge

Identify one item that could be sorted or searched, such as attractions by cost, location or type.

Exit Ticket

Name two characteristics that could describe a collection of Edinburgh attractions.
