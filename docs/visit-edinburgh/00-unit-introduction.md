# :material-web: Visit Edinburgh

Welcome to the ==Visit Edinburgh== website development unit.

In this unit, you will plan, design, build, test, and evaluate a one-page website about Edinburgh. Your website should help a chosen audience find useful information before visiting the city.

You will use RocketCake to build the website, so the focus is on good design choices rather than writing code.

---

By the end of the unit, you will create a one-page website called `Visit Edinburgh`.

## :compass: What You Will Learn

| Skill | What This Means |
|---|---|
| Planning | Decide the audience, purpose, sections, and order of your page before building. |
| Research | Find useful information and record where it came from. |
| Information Structure | Organise information into clear sections so visitors can find and compare information easily. |
| Design | Create a wireframe so you know where headings, text, images, and sections will go. |
| Website Build | Use RocketCake tools to create a clear one-page website. |
| Testing | Check that your website is readable, accessible, and suitable for visitors. |
| Evaluation | Explain what worked well and what you improved. |

## :material-map-marker-outline: Website Theme

Your website should help a specific audience explore Edinburgh. Some pupils may design a general visitor guide, while others may focus on a particular type of visit.

You might choose an audience such as:

- tourists and first-time visitors
- football or rugby fans
- theatre audiences
- music fans
- people attending concerts or festivals
- families looking for activities
- visitors who need transport, accessibility, or safety information

Your sections should match your audience. For example, a sports fan website might include stadium information, transport, food nearby, and match day tips. A theatre or music website might include venues, event information, travel options, food nearby, and accessibility details.

You do not need to include every idea. Choose the sections that best support your audience and purpose.

## :material-comment-check-outline: Before You Begin

Think about these questions:

1. Who is the website for?
2. What will your audience need to know first?
3. Which Edinburgh information will be most useful?
4. How can images make the page more helpful?
5. How will you know if your website helps your audience successfully?
