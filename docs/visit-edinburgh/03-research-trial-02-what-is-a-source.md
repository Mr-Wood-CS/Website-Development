# :material-map-marker-question-outline: Research Trial 2 - What Is A Source?

## :dart: Today You Will

Understand what a **source** is.

A source is **where information or an image came from**.

If you found a fact on a website, the website is your source.

If you found an image on a museum page, the museum page is your image source.

---

## :material-eye-outline: Examples Of Sources

A source could be:

- A website
- A map
- A museum page
- A transport page
- An official tourism page
- A photo website

| Fact Or Image | Source |
|-----------|-----------|
| A fact about Edinburgh Castle | VisitScotland website |
| Opening times for a museum | The museum website |
| A tram route map | Edinburgh Trams website |
| A photo of an attraction | The website where the photo was found |

---

## :material-alert-circle-outline: Source Or Not?

| Example | Is It A Source? | Why? |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| VisitScotland website | Yes | It is where the information was found. |
| "My friend said it" | Not a good source | It may not be accurate or easy to check. |
| National Museum of Scotland website | Yes | It is the official museum website. |
| "I think it is true" | No | It does not show where the information came from. |
