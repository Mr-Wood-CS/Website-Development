# :material-magnify: Research Trial 1 - What Is Research?

## :dart: Today You Will

Understand what **research** means for your `Visit Edinburgh` website.

## :material-brain: Quick Recall

Without looking at your notes, answer these questions.

1. What is research?
2. Why do people carry out research?
3. Name one thing a tourist might want to know about Edinburgh.

Don't worry if you are unsure. We will learn about this next.

Research means **looking for useful information before you make something**.

For this website, research means finding facts that would help someone visiting Edinburgh.

You are not trying to find everything about Edinburgh. You are choosing information that helps your visitor.

---

## :material-account-school: Model Answer

### Question

What does research mean when you are making a visitor website?

### Example Answer

Research means finding useful information before creating a website. For a Visit Edinburgh website, I could research attractions, transport, and places to visit. This helps visitors decide where to go and what to do.

### Why Is This A Good Answer?

✔ It explains what research means.

✔ It gives examples of information that could be researched.

✔ It explains why the information is useful.

---

## :material-eye-outline: Example

Imagine your website is for tourists visiting Edinburgh for the first time.

| Not Very Useful | More Useful |
|-----------|-----------|
| Edinburgh is nice. | Edinburgh Castle has exhibitions, city views, and important Scottish history. |
| There are things to do. | The National Museum of Scotland has galleries about nature, science, history, and world cultures. |
| You can travel around. | Edinburgh has buses and trams that help visitors travel around the city. |

Useful research helps the visitor know what to see, where to go, or what to do.
