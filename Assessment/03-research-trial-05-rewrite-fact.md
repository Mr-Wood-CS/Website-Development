# :material-pencil-outline: Research Trial 5 - Rewrite The Fact

## :dart: Today You Will

Rewrite one fact in your own words.

Rewrite means **say the same idea in your own way**.

You should not copy a whole sentence straight from a website.

---

## :material-eye-outline: Example

| Source Fact | Rewritten For A Website |
|-----------|-----------|
| Edinburgh Castle is located on Castle Rock and is one of Scotland's most famous historic sites. | Edinburgh Castle sits high on Castle Rock and is one of Scotland's most famous historic places to visit. |

The rewritten version keeps the meaning, but it sounds like it belongs on your own website.

---

## :material-lightbulb-outline: How To Rewrite

Try this:

1. Read the fact.
2. Cover the original sentence.
3. Say the idea out loud in simpler words.
4. Write your own sentence.
5. Check that the meaning is still correct.

---
