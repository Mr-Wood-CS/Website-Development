# :material-link-variant: Research Trial 3 - Why Save The Source URL?

## :dart: Today You Will

Understand why you need to save the **source URL**.

The source URL is the **web address of the page you used**.

It usually starts with `https://`.

Example:

`https://www.visitscotland.com/`

---

## :material-help-circle-outline: Why Do We Need It?

You save the source URL so you can:

- Find the same information again later.
- Prove where your fact came from.
- Check that the fact was not guessed.
- Give credit for information or images.
- Fix mistakes if the information changes.

Think of it like writing down where you found something. If you do not save the address, you may not be able to find it again.

---

## :material-eye-outline: Example

| What I Found | What I Need To Save |
|-----------|-----------|
| A fact about Edinburgh Castle | The website name and exact web address |
| A museum image | The museum page where the image came from |
| Transport information | The transport website URL |

The source website tells you **who or what gave you the information**.

The source URL tells you **exactly where to find it again**.

---

## :material-cursor-text: Where Do I Find The URL?

Look at the top of the browser window.

The URL is in the address bar.

Copy the full address from the address bar and paste it into your research table.
