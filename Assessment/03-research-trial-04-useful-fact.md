# :material-text-search: Research Trial 4 - Find One Useful Fact

## :dart: Today You Will

Find **one useful fact** for your `Visit Edinburgh` website.

A useful fact helps the visitor understand something, choose something, or plan something.

---

## :material-eye-outline: Weak Facts And Better Facts

| Weak Fact | Why It Is Weak | Better Fact |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Edinburgh Castle is good. | It does not explain why. | Edinburgh Castle has exhibitions, city views, and important Scottish history. |
| The museum is big. | It is too vague. | The National Museum of Scotland has galleries about nature, science, history, and world cultures. |
| There are buses. | It does not give enough detail. | Edinburgh has buses and trams that help visitors travel around the city. |

Better facts give the visitor useful detail.

---

## :material-account-question-outline: Ask Yourself

When you find a fact, ask:

- Would this help my visitor?
- Does it tell them something useful?
- Is it clear?
- Is it specific enough?

---

## :memo: Build Your Answer

**Question:** What makes a fact useful for a visitor?

Use each row to build one clear answer.

| My Answer Needs To | Words I Could Use |
|-----------|-----------|
| Say what a useful fact does | A useful fact helps the visitor understand, choose, or plan something. |
| Say what detail it gives | It gives clear detail instead of a short opinion. |
| Give examples | It could tell visitors what they can see, where they can go, or how they can travel. |
| Say why it matters | This helps visitors make better decisions. |
